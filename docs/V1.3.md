# Veyrix V1.3 - Multi-Market Trading Lab

Veyrix V1.3 expands the paper terminal and backtesting workspace to BTC, ETH, and SOL while preserving one shared $10,000 virtual-USDC account.

## Reference markets

Coinbase Exchange public `BTC-USD`, `ETH-USD`, and `SOL-USD` products provide reference prices and candles. Veyrix clearly identifies these as USD reference feeds; all simulated settlement remains virtual USDC.

## Trading model

- One open Spot Paper position per market; no spot shorts or averaging in.
- One isolated Futures Paper position per market with 1x, 2x, 3x, or 5x leverage, simulated fees, stops, targets, and liquidation.
- Margin and spot purchases reserve funds from the same virtual balance.
- Changing the viewed market never closes a position. The open-position bar links back to each market and mode.
- One autonomous configuration can run at a time. Its market, mode, strategy, timeframe, leverage, allocation, stop, and target are explicit.

## Compatibility

V1.2 trades without a market ID migrate to SOL. SOL spot holdings, futures history, automation settings, balances, and research remain readable. Research fingerprints include the selected market for new V1.3 runs.

## Safety

No exchange account, private credential, transaction signing, wallet funding, deposit, withdrawal, borrowing, or real order path exists. Every order, balance, fee, and liquidation is simulated.

## Recovery and validation

The shutdown left the V1.3 implementation and eight new test files uncommitted on top of V1.2 (9ad35a1). Recovery retained that implementation and repaired market-switch request isolation, per-market price observations, shared-account analytics, daily spot loss enforcement, SOL settlement/restoration, legacy execution-ID replay protection, research labels, and accidental dependency-version edits in the lockfile.

V1.3 includes 56 new deterministic tests. All 51 existing V1.2 test files (422 tests) remain unchanged; the combined suite contains 478 tests. Release validation uses lint, TypeScript, the full test suite, and the production build.

## Known simulation limits

- One autonomous configuration runs at a time in the open browser tab. Risk checks use polled prices; they do not run while the app is closed and cannot guarantee fills at a stop threshold.
- Coinbase USD prices are references, not executable USDC exchange quotes. Unavailable marks fall back to entry prices in account summaries.
- Futures use isolated simulated margin, simplified liquidation, and a 0.06% entry-notional fee charged on non-liquidation closure for new V1.3 trades. Legacy V1.2 positions retain their prior fee behavior. No funding or order-book model is provided.
- Market selection covers the live terminal, historical backtest/strategy comparison, and reproducible research library. Older dedicated SOL research tools remain SOL-specific; cross-market portfolio backtests and simultaneous autonomous agents are outside V1.3.
