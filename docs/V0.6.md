# Veyrix V0.6 analytics and backtesting

Veyrix remains paper-trading only. The backtest is a pure, isolated in-memory simulation and never reads or writes the live paper portfolio or its localStorage key.

## Historical data

- Source: Coinbase Exchange public `SOL-USD` product candles endpoint.
- Timeframe: trailing 7 days.
- Interval: 1-hour candles; the close is normalized as the strategy observation and simulated execution price.
- Retrieval: one server-side request, cached in memory for 15 minutes. No API secret is used or exposed.
- Provider constraint: Coinbase permits at most 300 candles per request, warns that historical buckets can be incomplete, and advises against frequent polling. Veyrix requests approximately 168 candles and exposes historical retrieval only when a user runs a backtest.

## Analytics definitions

- Net profit and total return use the mark-to-market current portfolio value, so they include an open position when present.
- Realized metrics, win/loss statistics, profit factor, expectancy, streaks, durations, the equity curve, and drawdown use only valid completed trades.
- Profit factor is gross positive realized P&L divided by the absolute gross negative realized P&L. It is unavailable when there are no completed losses or no completed trades.
- Dollar expectancy is `(win rate × average win) − (loss rate × absolute average loss)` per completed trade.
- Percentage expectancy is dollar expectancy divided by the average capital allocated per completed trade, multiplied by 100. It is unavailable if valid allocation data is missing.
- The realized equity curve begins at the configured starting balance and adds closed-trade P&L in exit-time order.
- Drawdown is measured from each realized-equity running peak. Break-even trades reset winning and losing streaks.

## Backtest assumptions

- No exchange fees or slippage.
- No liquidity or latency modeling.
- One long position maximum; no leverage, margin, or short selling.
- The existing Momentum Strategy evaluates observations chronologically.
- Stop-loss and take-profit checks use the same shared risk logic as live paper trading, evaluated at each candle close.
- Open positions are marked to the final observation and are not forcibly closed at period end.
- Results are historical simulations and should not be interpreted as predictions.
