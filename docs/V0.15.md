# Veyrix V0.15 — Research Workspace & Strategy Report

Veyrix remains a paper-trading and historical-simulation laboratory. It does not connect wallets, move funds, submit orders, use leverage, or provide investment advice. V0.15 changes research presentation and stored metadata only; trading strategies, backtests, benchmarks, and research formulas are unchanged.

## Research Workspace

`/research` is the dedicated workspace. The dashboard now stays focused on live SOL observations, the paper account, risk, agent state, trades, and live-account analytics. Navigation uses accessible Next.js links between Dashboard and Research.

The workspace organizes stored STANDARD research details into Overview, Strategies, Robustness, Regimes, Validation, Failure Analysis, Research Evidence, and Artifacts. Existing backtesting, robustness, multi-regime, extended-validation, and reproducible-research controls remain available under explicit experiment sections rather than forming one undifferentiated dashboard column. The timeline and initial report use summary/standard responses only; heavy artifacts remain lazy.

## Deterministic summary and report

`researchReport.ts` is a pure stored-data transformer. It does not import or call historical retrieval, parameter optimization, or research execution. A report records `generatedFromStoredData: true` and contains the stored run ID, historical range, assumptions, executive summary, category winners, scorecards, regime matrix, evidence card, failure profiles, conclusion, suggested next experiments, and mandatory simulation disclaimer.

Statements are rule templates filled with finite stored metrics. Positive-performance wording is used only when aggregate simulated OOS return is above zero. Unsupported regime/benchmark intersections are reported as unavailable rather than inferred. Markdown export and the compact shareable JSON snapshot are deterministic and contain no live paper state.

## Performance versus evidence

Performance Quality is deliberately separate from Validation Confidence:

- `STRONG`: aggregate OOS return above 0%, profitable-fold rate at least 55%, and matched-benchmark beat rate at least 50%.
- `MODERATE`: return is non-negative, or profitable-fold rate is at least 45%, or matched beat rate is at least 45%.
- `WEAK`: none of the above.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: a required metric is unavailable.

Evidence Quality reuses the unchanged V0.11 Validation Confidence: `LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGHER`, or `UNAVAILABLE`. Strong evidence can therefore accompany weak historical performance.

## Strategy verdicts

Verdicts are experimental research labels, never trade signals:

- `PROMISING`: OOS return above 0%, profitable-fold rate at least 55%, matched beat rate at least 50%, robustness at least 60, high-overfitting frequency at most 30%, confidence MODERATE/HIGHER, and reliability STRONG/PROMISING.
- `WEAK`: negative OOS return with profitable and matched-beat rates below 40%, or robustness below 35.
- `REGIME_DEPENDENT`: supported regimes include both positive and negative average OOS returns with at least a one-percentage-point range, after the WEAK rule.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: missing inputs or no other rule applies.

No verdict uses BUY, SELL, safe, guaranteed, or investment language.

## Regime story and failure profile

The matrix shows each strategy’s stored average OOS return and observed-window count for Bullish, Bearish, Sideways, and High Volatility regimes. At least two windows are required for an available cell. V0.14 STANDARD data does not retain a direct regime-to-matched-benchmark intersection, so that field is truthfully unavailable rather than reconstructed from unrelated aggregates.

Failure profiles aggregate the retained worst OOS folds: realized losses by STOP_LOSS, STRATEGY_SIGNAL, and TAKE_PROFIT, simulated fees, slippage, transaction-cost share, weakest supported regime, worst fold, and matched-benchmark frequency. Descriptions identify associations and observed rankings; they do not claim causation. Longest losing streak remains unavailable where STANDARD artifacts do not retain the necessary sequence.

## Evidence Card and conclusion drift

The Evidence Card shows historical days and data coverage, independent non-overlapping OOS folds, validation-method count, observed regimes, cross-method agreement, anchor consistency when present, Research Score, and Validation Confidence.

Conclusion Drift compares at least three summary records chronologically, including performance, robustness, and evidence leaders when retained by the summary:

- `STABLE`: no top-strategy or reliability changes and matched-beat-rate range at most 15 points.
- `CHANGING`: top-strategy and reliability change rates are each at most 50%, and matched-beat-rate range is at most 30 points.
- `HIGHLY_VARIABLE`: otherwise.
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: fewer than three summaries.

Only fields actually present in compact summaries participate. Robustness/evidence-leader drift is not fabricated for legacy summaries that do not retain those leader IDs.

## Notes, exports, and presentation mode

Each stored V2 run may hold optional strategy notes of at most 200 trimmed characters per registered strategy. They are plain metadata, saved only when durable storage is configured, and excluded from run identity and result fingerprints.

The report exports Markdown. A lightweight JSON snapshot exports run identity, historical range, ranking IDs, headline metrics, verdicts, confidence, benchmark wording, and the simulation disclaimer—never artifacts or live-account state.

Presentation mode is a compact factual product view: current SOL market observation, strategies tested, stored historical coverage, top current research finding, confidence, and an explicit paper-only label. It does not change or rerun research.

## Known limitations

Reports summarize retained STANDARD data, so artifact-only trade sequences and regime-specific benchmark intersections remain unavailable until explicitly hydrated. Conclusion Drift is descriptive and may have few saved runs. Strategy notes require the optional filesystem store and filesystem durability is deployment-dependent. Verdict thresholds are transparent heuristics, not statistical tests. Historical simulations and research evidence do not predict future performance.
