# Veyrix V0.11 — Extended Validation Lab

Veyrix remains paper-trading and historical simulation only. No wallets, private keys, exchange orders, blockchain transactions, leverage, margin, short selling, or real funds are involved. Historical evidence does not predict future performance.

## Extended historical retrieval

Coinbase Exchange SOL-USD OHLCV remains the source. All candles use a supported six-hour (`21600`) granularity. Retrieval is server-side, sequential, and cached as a complete dataset per timeframe for 15 minutes.

| History | Approximate candles | Requests |
|---|---:|---:|
| 90D | 361 | 2 |
| 180D | 721 | 3 |
| 365D | 1,461 | 5 |

A request spans no more than 299 intervals, yielding at most 300 boundary-inclusive candles. Adjacent requests share one pagination boundary. The merge validates OHLC values, records invalid removals, removes duplicate timestamps, sorts chronologically, and records missing intervals. The full dataset is fetched once; strategies and validation folds reuse it in memory.

## Historical data quality

Every API dataset reports expected approximate candles, actual valid candles, missing interval count, duplicates removed, invalid candles removed, first/last timestamps, and coverage percentage:

`coverage = clamp(actual valid candles / expected approximate candles × 100, 0, 100)`

Missing intervals are counted from gaps larger than one configured six-hour step. Incomplete history is therefore visible rather than silently treated as perfect.

## Validation methods

### Rolling Overlapping

Preserves V0.10: 121-candle windows advance by 40 candles. Each window splits chronologically into 84 in-sample and 37 OOS candles. These folds offer more observations but share data and are not independent.

### Non-Overlapping

Each independent unit contains 121 training candles, one embargo candle, and 40 future test candles. The next unit begins only after the prior test ends. Neither training nor test candles are reused between independent units, and test folds never overlap.

### Expanding Walk-Forward

Initial training contains 121 candles, followed by one embargo candle and 40 future test candles. The next fold expands training through the prior test history, inserts a new embargo candle, and evaluates the next non-overlapping 40-candle test block. Test results never influence earlier parameter selection.

For all methods, parameter sensitivity runs only on training data. The highest in-sample Veyrix Score configuration is frozen before the corresponding OOS test. Regime classification uses only that fold's test candles.

## Embargo

The centralized default is one candle—six hours. The candle immediately after training is excluded from both optimization and OOS measurement. It may enter a later expanding training history after its original fold has completed, but never its adjacent test.

## Cross-method agreement

Validation Agreement is experimental and bounded 0–100. At least two complete method summaries are required.

- Return similarity: `clamp(100 - 20 × range of average OOS returns)`
- Profitable-fold similarity: `clamp(100 - range of profitable-fold percentages)`
- Matched-beat similarity: `clamp(100 - range of matched benchmark beat rates)`
- Robustness similarity: `clamp(100 - range of average robustness scores)`
- Agreement: 30% return similarity + 25% profitable-fold similarity + 20% matched-beat similarity + 25% robustness similarity.

No one component has more than 30% weight.

## Exposure and failure analytics

Exposure is measured candle-by-candle using simulated entry and exit timestamps. Reports include percentage LONG/FLAT, average and maximum deployed capital, average exposure percentage, and experimental Return per 10% Market Exposure:

`strategy return % / (average exposure % / 10)`

Zero exposure returns an unavailable value.

Failure analysis ranks the three lowest OOS folds. It reports regime, market return, parameters, strategy and matched returns, drawdown, trades, losing trades, exit reasons, costs, exposure, stability, and overfitting risk. Explanations are deterministic and emitted only when supported—for example low-exposure bullish underperformance, stop-loss-dominated volatile losses, high transaction-cost share, or unstable neighboring parameters.

Loss attribution groups absolute realized losses by `STOP_LOSS`, `STRATEGY_SIGNAL`, and `TAKE_PROFIT`. Fee/slippage loss share is `(fees + estimated slippage) / absolute realized losses`, clamped to 0–100.

## Regime duration and transitions

The V0.10 classifier is applied to chronological 40-candle blocks. Adjacent identical regimes merge into a duration containing start/end, candle/day duration, market return, volatility, strategy trades/return, and full-SOL benchmark return. Transitions describe the following regime block's strategy return and trades. They are descriptive and do not imply causation.

## Research Score and confidence

The experimental Veyrix Research Score measures evidence quality—not profitability:

- 30% Validation Agreement
- 25% historical coverage percentage
- 20% independent-fold coverage, saturated at eight folds
- 15% observed regime coverage out of four regimes
- 10% low-overfitting evidence (`100 - average high-overfitting frequency`)

Validation Confidence:

- `HIGHER`: at least 180D, ≥90% data coverage, ≥6 independent folds, ≥3 regimes, and agreement ≥70.
- `MODERATE`: at least 90D, ≥75% coverage, ≥3 independent folds, ≥2 regimes, and agreement ≥50.
- `LOW`: valid evidence below those thresholds.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: required evidence is missing.

“Higher” deliberately avoids claims of statistical significance.

## Ranking separation

- Performance Ranking uses mean OOS return across validation methods.
- Robustness Ranking uses the existing Multi-Regime Robustness Score.
- Research Confidence Ranking uses Veyrix Research Score.

## Limitations

Overlapping folds are not independent. Non-overlapping folds provide fewer observations. Expanding training can overweight older regimes. Regime blocks and thresholds are heuristic. Even 365 days may omit relevant long-cycle conditions. Six-hour OHLC data hides intrabar paths, and execution omits variable spreads, liquidity, latency, market impact, partial fills, taxes, and changing fee tiers. Optimization can still overfit despite embargoes and future-only testing.
