# Veyrix V0.7 realistic backtesting

Veyrix remains paper-trading and simulation only. V0.7 does not connect wallets, submit exchange or blockchain orders, or support leverage, margin, or short selling. Live paper trading retains the V0.6 zero-fee execution model and persisted trade schema; V0.7 transaction costs apply only to isolated backtests and benchmarks.

## Historical candles

Coinbase Exchange public SOL-USD candles preserve timestamp, open, high, low, close, and volume. Invalid timestamps, non-positive or non-finite prices, impossible OHLC relationships, malformed rows, and duplicate timestamps are skipped. Momentum still evaluates chronological close-price observations.

| Period | Coinbase granularity | Expected maximum |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 7D | 1 hour (3600) | 169 |
| 14D | 6 hours (21600) | 57 |
| 30D | 6 hours (21600) | 121 |

Coinbase Exchange does not support a 2-hour candle. Each selection uses one server-side request, remains below the 300-candle limit, and is cached independently for 15 minutes.

## Execution model

- Existing long positions are stopped when candle low is at or below the stop and reach their target when candle high is at or above the target.
- OHLC data cannot reveal intrabar ordering. When both levels are touched, the centralized STOP_FIRST default conservatively selects the stop. TARGET_FIRST exists as an internal extensibility option.
- Entry execution is the signal close increased by 0.05% deterministic adverse slippage.
- Strategy exits are the signal close decreased by 0.05%.
- Stop and target exits use their trigger level decreased by 0.05%.
- Each entry and exit charges 0.10% of executed notional.
- Gross P&L uses ideal prices. Net P&L subtracts entry and exit slippage cost and both fees exactly once.
- Risk levels use the simulated executed entry price.
- No liquidity, order-book depth, latency, or exact intrabar-path modeling is attempted.

## Buy-and-hold comparison

The benchmark commits the full $10,000 at the first valid close, applies the same entry fee and slippage, holds SOL for the selected period, and applies the same hypothetical exit costs at each mark and at the final close. This provides a cost-consistent liquidation-value equity curve.

Return Difference = Momentum Agent return % minus Buy & Hold SOL return %.

This is a simple historical return differential, not CAPM or Jensen's alpha. Historical simulation results do not predict future performance.

## Architecture

- marketCandles.ts: timeframe mapping and OHLC validation.
- historicalMarketService.ts: bounded server-side Coinbase retrieval and caching.
- executionModel.ts: fees, slippage, and intrabar collision handling.
- backtestEngine.ts: isolated Momentum Agent portfolio simulation.
- benchmarkEngine.ts: isolated full-capital SOL benchmark and return comparison.
- performanceAnalytics.ts: shared trade analytics and drawdown calculations.
